Our Approach

How we help you think like an analyst.

01

Real Scenarios

Every case is grounded in an actual business situation, written the way real requests land on an analyst's desk — not an abstract textbook problem.

02

An Analyst's Framework

Each case comes with the step-by-step thinking a senior analyst would actually use — so you learn the process, not just the final number.

03

Self-Assessment

Work the case on your own first, then reveal the guided solution outline to check your reasoning and spot what you missed.

04

Free, Always Growing

Case Lab stays free for anyone starting out in finance, with new cases added over time across valuation, FP&A, and modeling.
